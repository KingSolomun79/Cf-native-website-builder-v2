## Inspiration Repositories Directory

### 1. Digital Innovator (SaaS, Tech, Dark Mode)
* **[Godly.website](https://godly.website/)**: Cutting-edge, animated, premium tech/SaaS sites.
* **[Dark Mode Design](https://www.darkmodedesign.com/)**: Beautiful dark-themed websites.
* **[SaaS Landing Page](https://saaslandingpage.com/)**: Software and tech startup landing pages.

### 2. Corporate Trust & Clinical Clean (Finance, Legal, Healthcare)
* **[Siteinspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/)**: Clean, minimal, and typography-driven websites.
* **[Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery/)**: Uncluttered websites utilizing whitespace effectively.
* **[Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja/)**: Massive directory of clean, conversion-focused landing pages.

### 3. Rugged Utility (Trades, Construction, Local Service)
* **[One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/)**: The ultimate showcase of single-page websites (perfect for simple "Call Now" trade sites).
* **[Land-book](https://land-book.com/)**: Great mix of local business landing pages and portfolios.

### 4. Immersive Visual & Storefront (Real Estate, Hospitality, Retail)
* **[Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/)**: Award-winning, highly interactive, and visually stunning sites.
* **[MaxiBestOf](https://maxibestof.one/)**: Elegant, typography-heavy, editorial-style websites.
* **[Commerce Cream](https://commercecream.com/)**: Beautiful e-commerce storefronts.

### 5. Component & Real-World UI Patterns
### 5. Component & Real-World UI Patterns
* **[Refero.design](https://refero.design/)**: Library of real-world design patterns broken down by element (footers, hero sections, etc).
* **[Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/)**: Massive web section for complex dashboards or intricate layouts.

### 6. General Style Discovery & Prompts
* **[Design Prompts](https://www.designprompts.dev/)**: AI-powered design style explorer with 31+ curated aesthetics and ready-to-use prompts to recreate them.
